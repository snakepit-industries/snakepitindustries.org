LEGACY HARDWARE

Age alone is not a failure condition.

A machine may remain useful because it is:

  > RELIABLE
  > REPAIRABLE
  > WELL DOCUMENTED
  > INTERFACE-RICH
  > OFFLINE CAPABLE
  > PURPOSE BUILT
  > INTERESTING
  > ALREADY PAID FOR

"OLD" and "USELESS" are not synonyms.

---------------------------------------------------------------

STORAGE RECOVERY

Old storage hardware may be inventoried separately from the
data recovered from it.

Typical workflow:

  [01] IDENTIFY MEDIA.

  [02] DOCUMENT CONDITION.

  [03] AVOID UNNECESSARY WRITES.

  [04] IMAGE WHEN PRACTICAL.

  [05] HASH THE IMAGE.

  [06] EXPLORE A COPY.

  [07] CATALOG INTERESTING ARTIFACTS.

  [08] PUT THE DRIVE SOMEWHERE "SAFE."

  [09] IMMEDIATELY FORGET WHICH SAFE PLACE WAS USED.

HARDWARE PHILOSOPHY

Use the right tool.

Understand the interfaces.

Keep the documentation.

Repair what makes sense to repair.

Preserve what deserves preservation.

And never throw away a weird adapter until you are absolutely
certain what it does.

Then wait another five years.
