---------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW

SONAR is an experimental passive radio-frequency awareness
system.

The concept is simple:

Observe nearby RF activity.

Record only what is necessary.

Correlate changes over time.

Build situational awareness without becoming the problem.

Think of it as:

WARDIVING IN REVERSE.

---------------------------------------------------------------

PRIMARY GOALS

  > PASSIVE RF OBSERVATION
  > LOCAL SIGNAL AWARENESS
  > DEVICE-PRESENCE PATTERNING
  > TELEMETRY
  > DISTRIBUTED SENSOR CONCEPTS
  > EVENT DETECTION
  > RF ENVIRONMENT BASELINING

---------------------------------------------------------------

LONGER-TERM CONCEPT

Multiple authorized sensors may eventually operate as a
distributed network.

Potential functions include:

  > COMPARING SIGNAL OBSERVATIONS
  > TELEMETRY RELAY
  > APPROXIMATE LOCATION ESTIMATION
  > EVENT CORRELATION
  > COMMUNITY SENSOR NODES

Nothing here requires the sensors to be chatty.

Quiet machines are often useful machines.
