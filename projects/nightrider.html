---------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW

Nightrider is a compact mobile computing system designed for
field use.

Primary considerations are:

  > PORTABILITY
  > CONNECTIVITY
  > PHYSICAL I/O
  > RADIO / ANTENNA SUPPORT
  > LOW POWER REQUIREMENTS
  > LOCAL NETWORK ACCESS
  > DURABILITY
  > CONCEALABILITY

It should be small enough to disappear into a bag.

It should be useful enough to justify bringing it.

---------------------------------------------------------------

DESIGN PRIORITIES

  [01] SMALL FORM FACTOR.

  [02] REAL PORTS ARE BETTER THAN DONGLES.

  [03] EXTERNAL ANTENNA OPTIONS ARE GOOD.

  [04] BATTERY LIFE MATTERS.

  [05] OFFLINE OPERATION SHOULD REMAIN USEFUL.

  [06] FIELD REPAIR SHOULD BE POSSIBLE.

  [07] THE MACHINE SHOULD NOT REQUIRE A CLOUD ACCOUNT TO BOOT.

  [08] BOOTING SHOULD LOOK RIDICULOUSLY COOL.
